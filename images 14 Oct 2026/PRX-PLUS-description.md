# Clinical Treatment Guide: PRX-PLUS Skin Booster & Brightening Gel

**PRX-PLUS** is an advanced, professional-grade, needle-free **Topical Biostimulator™** designed to deeply rejuvenate skin, boost elasticity, and eliminate severe hyperpigmentation. Unlike traditional chemical peels, PRX-PLUS penetrates straight to the dermal layer to trigger cellular renewal and collagen creation **without surface peeling, frosting, or downtime**.

---

## 🔬 Core Ingredients & Mechanism
* **33% Ammonium Trichloroacetate (ATCA):** The stable salt form of TCA. It bypasses epidermal injury to target the dermis directly, stimulating fibroblasts to rapidly amplify collagen and elastin production.
* **2% Homocysteic Acid (HoA):** Accelerates cellular turnover and draws embedded melanin out of melanocytes to naturally eliminate stubborn pigment.
* **Citric & Phytic Acid:** Gently exfoliates dead surface cells and acts as a tyrosinase inhibitor to block the formation of future dark spots.

---

## ✨ Primary Benefits
* **Advanced Brightening:** Erases stubborn dark spots, photodamage, uneven skin tone, and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation.
* **No-Needle "Liquid Lift":** Plumps out fine lines and deep wrinkles while correcting skin laxity without injections or recovery time.
* **Textural Refinement:** Noticeably minimizes the appearance of enlarged pores and smooths irregular skin textures.
* **Universal Application:** Safe for all skin types (Fitzpatrick I–VI) and can be performed year-round, including during the summer.

## 📊 PRX-PLUS vs. Traditional PRX-T33

| Feature | PRX-PLUS | PRX-T33 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Formula** | 33% Ammonium TCA, 2% Homocysteic Acid, Citric & Phytic Acid | 33% TCA, Hydrogen Peroxide ($H_2O_2$), 5% Kojic Acid |
| **Primary Focus** | Deep wrinkles, severe hyperpigmentation, skin laxity, and enlarged pores | Fine lines, early-stage skin elastosis, fresh acne scars, and stretch marks |
| **Brightening Power** | Intense (Direct cellular melanin extraction) | Moderate (Tyrosinase inhibition) |
| **Best Suited For** | Photodamaged, mature, or thick skin | Sensitive skin types or younger complexions |

## ⏱️ Treatment Protocol & Homecare
* **Application Method:** Applied exclusively by a certified professional using a specialized, firm topical massage technique. (Duration: 10–15 minutes).
* **Recommended Course:** A base series of **4 sessions spaced 2 weeks apart** for optimal clinical outcomes.
* **Maintenance:** A single session every 45 days, or repeating the full treatment course twice a year.
* **Essential Homecare:** Client must use a nourishing barrier-repair cream (such as WiQo Nourishing and Moisturizing Cream) post-procedure to protect and hydrate the skin's hydrolipidic film.
